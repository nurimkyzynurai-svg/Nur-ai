# Viply Roadmap

## Core Features

| # | Feature | Stage |
|---|---------|-------|
| 1 | Feedback Loop | MVP |
| 2 | Messenger Approval | MVP |
| 3 | Viral Video Breakdown | Later |
| 4 | One Thought to Content | Later |
| 5 | Pre-Publish Score | Later |

### 1. Feedback Loop (MVP)

The AI learns from every edit the client makes to a script and remembers their preferences. Over time, edits become rare.

### 2. Messenger Approval (MVP)

Finished videos are sent to the client in Telegram or WhatsApp with three buttons: **Publish**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

### 3. Viral Video Breakdown (Later)

The client pastes a link to a viral video. The AI explains why it worked (hook, pacing, emotion), then creates a similar original video in the client's style.

### 4. One Thought to Content (Later)

The client records a 1-minute voice note. Viply turns it into a Reel, a post, a carousel, a Threads post and an article.

### 5. Pre-Publish Score (Later)

Before publishing, the AI rates the video's viral potential from 1 to 10 and suggests improvements.

## Brand DNA

Brand DNA is the client's voice profile. It's created during onboarding in a step called **Voice Setup**:

1. **Upload best posts:** the client uploads 3–5 of their best-performing posts.
2. **Answer 5 questions:**
   - **Tone:** how should the content sound?
   - **Style:** what format and visual style do they prefer?
   - **Audience:** who are they speaking to?
   - **Favorite phrases:** words and expressions they use often.
   - **Never do:** topics, words or styles to always avoid.

All AI agents use this profile, so every hook, script, caption and video matches the client's voice.
